About us

The start of our adventure

Our company was founded back in 1954, a time where dramatic shipwrecks and accidents resulting in loss of life was a fairly regular occurrence in towns and villages up and down the coast. Such tragic experiences awakened an interest in developing products that could improve safety at sea.
