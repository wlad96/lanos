Sertificats

Our network of agents ensures that we are represented in most shipbuilding markets worldwide and our service team is at hand to assist you whenever necessary. Feel free to contact our head office or an agent near you.

Technology

Advanced manufacturing capabilities

Through state-of-the-art robotic infrastructure and certified metallurgical processes, we ensure absolute dimensional accuracy and marine-grade integrity for every single component.

  • Safety First

    Every hatch and door undergoes pressure tests and hydrostatic integrity validations far beyond minimum class regulations.

  • Innovation

    We invest relentlessly in advanced alloy technologies and hybrid composite materials for lighter, stronger installations.

  • Sustainability

    Our factory runs entirely on clean hydroelectricity and leverages up to 80% recycled aluminum in our marine doors.
